A Note from Mom & Dad

To our family and friends,

Eleanor Rae is a gift from God, and we are deeply grateful for the love and generosity you have already shown her.

Our hope is to raise Ellie to love God, love her neighbor, and live with humility, wisdom, and integrity. We pray she would grow into a woman who sees her life, gifts, and opportunities not simply as things for herself, but as ways to serve others faithfully.

As we think about her education and future, we want to steward well what has been entrusted to her. We care far more about wisdom, character, and preparation for a meaningful life than prestige alone.

We will prioritize value over appearance, and seek the path that best equips her to think clearly, live faithfully, and serve others well. Our goal is not simply to spend on her future, but to steward it carefully so that every gift given toward it can go as far as possible.

We are already praying over this account, that God would use it for her good, shape her into a faithful woman, and guide her future in ways that honor Him.

Your gift is an investment in her future, and we do not take that lightly.

Thank you for loving Ellie and for walking alongside our family.
